Addiction & Recovery

Education, Healing & Sustainable Change

Addiction and recovery are often approached through a lens of lifelong management—focusing heavily on managing urges, suppressing cravings, and coping with emotions as they arise. While management has its place, true transformation requires looking deeper. Rather than just teaching you how to manage the symptoms, my work focuses on healing the underlying belief systems, automatic patterns, and emotional drivers that generate those urges and cravings in the first place, helping to calm intrusive thoughts and feelings at their root.

Serving the South Jersey and Philadelphia area—including Camden County, Gloucester County, Burlington County, and Atlantic County. I offer specialized educational services and private consultations designed for individuals, groups, and organizations looking to step beyond traditional treatment models.

Group Presentations & Organizational Education

For organizations, recovery groups, and community gatherings across our local area, I offer educational presentations and workshops that break down the mechanics of habit loops, behavioral patterns, and the systems of addiction. These sessions provide an in-depth, educational perspective designed to shift how groups understand behavior, change, and the recovery process.

Private Services for Individuals

Whether you are seeking private guidance for yourself or looking for an approach that goes beyond standard rehab protocols, I work with individuals at every stage of the recovery journey:

  • Contemplation & Planning: If you are trying to decide how to address your addiction and figure out your next steps, I provide a structured, educational space to explore your options and clarify your path forward.
  • Action & In-Depth Learning: If you have taken action but find yourself looking for something taught beyond traditional rehab—such as in-depth education, applying hypnosis and mindfulness in addiction and recovery, or needing practical help with motivation, handling urges, and structuring your time and daily life—we work directly on rebuilding those foundational patterns.
  • Maintenance & Emotional Healing: If you are already in maintenance and need help addressing lingering emotional concerns, clearing out old mental associations, and securing long-term stability, we focus on deep-level resolution rather than mere maintenance.
