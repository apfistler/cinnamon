Groups & Events: Educational Materials

This section contains educational materials and resources associated with groups, workshops, and events I am currently conducting or have conducted in the past. Over the years, I have had the opportunity to share these materials with individuals and groups throughout the South Jersey and Philadelphia areas, covering topics such as addiction and recovery, mindfulness, meditation, self-talk, behavioral change, and personal growth.

These resources include handouts, exercises, guides, and other materials that were developed or shared as part of those programs. Some were created for a specific group or event, while others are designed to stand on their own and may be useful beyond the setting in which they were originally presented. I will continue adding materials as I develop new resources and revisit the work from groups and events I have conducted throughout the South Jersey and Philadelphia communities.
