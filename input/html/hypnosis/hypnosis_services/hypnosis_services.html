How Does My Hypnosis Process Work?

My approach to hypnosis focuses on resolving the root causes behind unwanted behaviors and emotional patterns—what is happening "under the hood." Rather than simply attempting to suppress a symptom, I systematically address the underlying beliefs, emotional triggers, and learned associations.

When you schedule your initial session, I begin with a brief preliminary conversation about what you would like to accomplish. This helps me establish the focus for our first meeting.

Your First Visit: Explanation & In-Depth Intake

When you arrive for your first appointment, I sit down directly with you to demystify the process. I explain exactly what hypnosis is, what it isn't, and address common misconceptions. You will understand that hypnosis is a collaborative, conscious state where you remain aware, in control, and active in your own change process.

Next, I gather comprehensive background information to map out the issue:

  • Your Goals: What success looks like to you and what you want to achieve.
  • Past Attempts: What you have tried in the past and where conscious effort or willpower fell short.
  • The Impact: How this issue is currently affecting your life—personally, emotionally, socially, and professionally.
  • The Meaning of Change: What making this change will mean for your future, as well as the personal cost of remaining stuck in the old pattern.

From this evaluation, I outline what hypnosis can realistically accomplish, how I will address the problem, and what your role in the process will be.

Phase 1: Establishing the Foundation

During your initial hypnosis work, my goal is to build a solid foundation for long-term success. I guide you into the appropriate level of hypnosis required for deep psychological work. Once the proper depth is achieved, I perform a convincer—demonstrating directly to your conscious mind that you are in hypnosis.

With the foundation set, I introduce initial reframing of existing blockages, build foundational motivation, and begin shifting the behavioral aspects of the issue. When you emerge, I review the experience with you, gather your feedback, and establish awareness goals for the week ahead.

Phase 2: Resolving Beliefs, Emotions, and Root Causes

In subsequent sessions, I move into the heart of the work. This is where I address the web of connections driving the behavior:

  • Uncovering and releasing limiting core belief systems (e.g., self-sabotage, unworthiness, or fear).
  • Separating false hunger, false cravings, or urges from underlying emotional triggers using The Secret Language of Feelings.
  • Resolving stored guilt, shame, anger, or resentment tied to the issue or past quit attempts.

By systematically resolving the emotional and psychological drivers, the need for the old behavior naturally dissipates.

Phase 3: Custom Motivation & Reinforcement

Because the underlying emotional drivers are cleared during the core work, many of my clients find that their habits have naturally shifted and require minimal additional push. However, if further structure, specific dietary/exercise motivation, or habit reinforcement is needed, I customize this final phase to fit your individual goals.
