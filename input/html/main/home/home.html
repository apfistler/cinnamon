Explore the Mind. Understand Yourself. Create Meaningful Change.

As a Behavioral Change Consultant, I help people explore the patterns that shape how they think, feel, behave, and experience their lives My work brings together practical tools and perspectives from hypnosis, mindfulness, meditation, addiction and recovery, behavior change, handwriting analysis, astrology, technology, and systems thinking.

At the center of all of this is a simple idea: understanding patterns gives us more choices about what we do next. Sometimes that means changing a habit or behavior. Sometimes it means gaining a different perspective, understanding yourself more clearly, or finding meaning in experiences that have been difficult to understand.

I offer several ways to explore these ideas. I can help you examine personal patterns and behavior, learn about hypnosis and mindfulness, explore addiction and recovery, or discover personal insights through handwriting analysis and astrology.

I also offer speaking, workshops, and interactive experiences for groups, organizations, parties, and other gatherings. These can range from educational presentations and discussions about behavior and the mind to something completely different from the usual entertainment.

My technology work comes from a different set of tools, but a similar way of thinking. With a background in systems engineering, software development, automation, and IT integration, I look for patterns in systems, understand how their parts interact, and find ways to make them work better together.

Whether you're looking for personal exploration, behavioral change, an unusual perspective, an engaging speaker or workshop facilitator, or help making a technology system work more effectively, the underlying approach is the same: understand the system, identify the patterns, and find opportunities for meaningful change.
